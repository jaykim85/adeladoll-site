About the Dolls

A short guide for anyone new to porcelain dolls.

01

Original Antique

Dolls more than a hundred years old, made in the doll factories of Germany and France between the 1880s and the 1910s. The maker's mark, mold number and size number are incised on the back of the neck.

02

Antique Reproduction

Dolls made by a present-day artist from a mold taken from an antique original. The face is the same, but the doll comes out slightly smaller after firing, and the artist's initials and the year are incised on the back of the head. Reproductions bring masterpieces that are too rare to see in person within reach.

03

Modern Artist Doll

Dolls made from an original sculpted by a contemporary artist. They carry the natural expression and proportions of a real person and the taste of today, and the sculptor and the maker who completed the doll are often different people.

Terms you will meet

Bisque
Porcelain fired without a glaze; its matte surface has the texture of skin.
Mold
The plaster form in which heads and limbs are cast. A mold number identifies the face; it is not a year.
DEP
Short for the French 'Depose' or the German 'Deponirt': a registered design.
Sleep eyes
Glass eyes that close when the doll is laid down and open when she is stood up, moved by a weight inside the head.
Composition body
A body made of a material of sawdust and glue, with ball joints strung with elastic so the doll can be posed.
Shoulder head / socket head
A head modeled down to the shoulders sits on a cloth or kid body; a head ending at the neck fits into a jointed body.
Dolly face / character doll
The 'dolly face' is the idealized pretty child; the character doll, from 1909, has a natural expression modeled on a real child.
Parian
German dolls of the 1860s-1880s in untinted white bisque, with the hair modeled in the porcelain.
D.A.G
The Doll Artisan Guild, an international organization of porcelain dollmakers founded in 1977, running seminars, certification and competitions.
Lady doll / bebe
A doll with an adult woman's figure is a lady (fashion) doll or poupee; a French doll with a child's face is a bebe.
